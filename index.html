How to practice

Study a card

Select your filters, then select Start practice. Try to recall all the forms for the verb and tense shown before tapping the card to reveal the answer.

Compare your answers, then rate the form you found hardest. Include both affirmative and negative forms for imperative cards.

Rate your recall

Again
You missed any form, made an error, or needed to look at the answer.
Hard
All forms were correct, but you struggled to recall at least one.
Good
All forms were correct with some hesitation.
Easy
All forms were correct immediately and confidently.

Hard, Good, and Easy all mean you answered correctly before revealing.

Move between cards

Previous and Next browse the remaining cards without rating them. Previous does not undo a rating or bring back a card you already rated.

Only rating a card advances the progress bar. It tracks the cards reviewed in the current round, not mastery. Cards that become due again start a new review round.

To change an active session, select Edit filters, choose your settings, then select Start practice to start a new round.

What is spaced repetition?

Spaced repetition brings cards back over time to help you remember them. Your ratings guide the next review: difficult cards generally return sooner, and easier cards later.

A rated card may disappear until it is due again. When you are caught up, the app shows the next review time for your selection.
